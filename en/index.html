    How we work

    Four clear steps, and you see progress yourself every week.

    1. 1

      Understand

      A call to learn about your idea, audience and goals. Then we send you the scope, cost and timeline.

    2. 2

      Design

      We build the brand, map the user journey and screens, and revise with you until you approve.

    3. 3

      Build & test

      We turn the design into code and show you a working version every week.

    4. 4

      Launch & support

      We ship the project and stay with you after launch for support and improvements.
